**Ultrawide screens and windowed mode.**

- **Fullscreen keeps the picture's shape.** If the game's resolution isn't your screen's shape (a 16:9 resolution on an ultrawide, for example), it's no longer stretched: the picture fills the screen's height, centred, with black bars at the sides. On a 5120x1440 or 3440x1440 screen, use fullscreen at **2560x1440**.
- **Windowed mode stays above the taskbar.** The window is centred in the part of the screen the taskbar leaves free, and made smaller (keeping its shape) if it doesn't fit.
- The game no longer moves its window back over these a moment after it starts.

Tested on a 16:9 screen (with other resolutions standing in for an ultrawide's shape). If you have an ultrawide, feedback is welcome.

**To install or update:** download `tsfixplus-1.1.0.zip` below (under Assets) and copy **`d3d9.dll`** and **`tsfixplus.ini`** into the game folder, replacing the old ones (in Steam: right-click Tales of Symphonia → Manage → Browse local files). Works on Windows and the Steam Deck. See the [README](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/ts-fix-plus#readme) for recommended settings.
